Introducing Custom Orthotics at Melrose Foot Care

We’re delighted to announce that Melrose Foot Care now offers custom-made orthotics to help support your foot health, boost comfort, and address a range of mobility concerns.

As part of our biomechanical assessments, we now use state-of-the-art Footscan® pressure plate technology. This allows us to analyse exactly how pressure is distributed across your feet when standing and walking. Combined with a thorough clinical assessment, our podiatrists can create a tailored orthotic prescription designed specifically for your feet.

We also offer semi-bespoke devices called Formthotics™. These are fantastic for enhancing shock absorption, reducing muscle fatigue, and improving balance—offering long-lasting comfort whether you’re an athlete, on your feet all day at work, or simply looking to move more comfortably.

Experiencing foot pain, recurring injuries or discomfort when walking or exercising? Get in touch with our friendly team to find out whether custom orthotics could benefit you!

Condition Focus - Parkinson's Disease

Tracy was invited to talk to our local group in September. The friendly group meets every month on the last Monday of the month, 2-4pm at Lucy Sanderson Hall in Galashiels.

If you or a family member are affected by Parkinson’s Disease the group is a great source of support, information and resources to help navigate the condition. As a special offer all new bookings that mention Borders Parkinson’s Disease Group will receive a free gift at their first appointment!

Parkinson's UK Local Support Finder

Five signs you or someone you support has Parkinson's Diseason would benefit from seeing a Podiatrist are:

1. Cuts, blisters or broken skin

2. Thickened nails

3. Swelling in feet, ankle and legs

4. Areas of pressure or rubbing from loose shoes

5. Muscle Pain and cramping that is new or worsening
